# ATOTSM — Version 2 Narrative Blueprint

## Central proposition

Understand the conditions we have inherited.
Discover the possibilities we have neglected.
Explore how we can create something better together.

atotsm means "a taste of the social media": an invitation to encounter a connected body of philosophical, intellectual, creative and practical work. It is not a demand to adopt an ideology, join a movement or agree with every conclusion.

The visitor should feel invited to explore, question, connect ideas and take what is useful.

## 1. The starting question

Many problems are treated as if they were unrelated.
Could some share underlying dynamics?
What assumptions, incentives and structures shape our possibilities?
What changes when we investigate roots as well as symptoms?

Present this as the inquiry motivating the work, not as a demand that visitors accept a predetermined conclusion.

## 2. Usurpia and Arcadia

These are not geographical places or separate societies.
They describe coexisting conditions within people and society.

- Usurpia: a condition in which the dynamics of usury and their consequences remain insufficiently recognised.
- Arcadia: a condition of increasing awareness, human possibility, cooperation and the creation of alternatives.

They coexist in the same world. A person, relationship or community can contain elements of both.

The Exodus is metaphorical: an awakening and movement towards alternatives here and now, not a physical migration away from society.

The invitation is to move from unawareness towards awareness and possibility.

## 3. Two mutually enriching disciplines

### Usurology

An interdisciplinary investigation of usury as a systemic phenomenon, including its structures, dynamics, consequences and persistent unawareness.

It investigates monetary structures and their wider personal, relational, societal, environmental, moral and other dimensions.

Its purpose is to investigate underlying dynamics rather than endlessly treating symptoms separately.

### Arcadialogy / Ikigaiology

An open philosophy of human development, purpose, talents, interests, knowledge, capabilities and flourishing.

Each person can cultivate an evolving personal library according to their own curiosity, direction and potential. There is no compulsory universal curriculum and no requirement to monetise every talent.

It explores how people can combine capabilities, create value, cooperate and develop alternatives.

The two disciplines are not consecutive stages. Understanding changes what we choose to create; practical experience changes how we understand the world.

## 4. The systemic investigation

Introduce the idea that usury is investigated as a wider systemic dynamic, not reduced to the ordinary question of high interest rates.

Relevant concepts to introduce progressively:
- Persistent Usury Unawareness.
- PPPPP: Promise, Pressure, Parasitism, Pathologization, Poison.
- The Lens and The Paradigms.
- The Flux and the Emergineering.
- The 7/8 Dimensional Impact.
- The Rocket Booster metaphor: acceleration can create possibilities, but balance and timely disengagement matter.

Do not attempt to explain every model on the homepage. Introduce the existence and purpose of the investigation, then invite deeper exploration in the Sovereign Library.

## 5. The human possibility

Arcadialogy / Ikigaiology is broader than critical thinking or career development.

It includes:
- Individual interests, talents, purpose and capabilities.
- Evolving personal libraries of knowledge and practice.
- Shared foundations such as logical fallacies, biases, emotional literacy, virtues, mythology, religions, philosophy, cultures and multiple intelligences.
- Practical skills, crafts, ecology, food production, technology and self-sufficiency.
- Freedom to learn and contribute without making every interest a job.
- Ways of recognising value beyond one universal measure.

## 6. From individual possibility to collective flourishing

Show an emergent pathway:

Individuals → Relationships → Communities → Society.

Individuals discover and cultivate capabilities.
Relationships connect different contributions.
Communities organise cooperation around actual needs and shared possibilities.
Wider change can emerge from flourishing relationships and communities.

This is not a centrally imposed blueprint.

Complementary currencies can help recognise and connect different forms of contribution, time, resources, skills and capabilities. They are part of a wider monetary pluralism and practical experimentation, not a single universal solution.

Other alternatives include peer-to-peer cooperation, open source, digital commons, cooperative structures, permaculture, food forests and self-organising initiatives.

## 7. The work ecosystem

### Sovereign Library — understanding and knowledge

The theoretical, diagnostic, historical, forensic, theological, evolutionary and communicative body of Usurology work.

Its seven divisions:
1. Foundational Maps.
2. Archive of Deception.
3. Forensic Instruments.
4. Medical & Somatic Interventions.
5. Theological & Evolutionary Shifts.
6. Jubilee Strategies.
7. Media House.

The homepage should introduce these divisions, not reproduce every entry.

### Sovereign Laboratory — practical exploration

The experimental and design collection associated with Arcadialogy / Ikigaiology.

Its four divisions:
1. Pedagogy of Play.
2. Currency Laboratory.
3. Digital Commons.
4. Somatic & Ecological Grounding.

Examples include Polypoly, complementary currency designs, Community Recovery Note, Bread Token, Festicoin, Bloom Protocol, Unyt Smart Agreements, Yutho, Holomonday, Cubic Foot Gardening and Soevereign.

These are interconnected experiments and projects, not claims that every proposal is already operational.

### AIology and applied research

Research into AI interaction, reasoning methodologies, human–AI collaboration and practical applications.

It includes methodological work and applied investigations, including the Bende van Nijvel cold-case research.

AI is an instrument for investigation, reasoning, experimentation and creation. It should not be presented as a replacement for human judgement.

## 8. Publishing and exploration

The homepage is the gateway, not the entire library.

Individual work can take the form of essays, books, visual explanations, interactive investigations, tools or standalone websites.

The visitor should be able to follow an idea into its own context and discover connections to other work.

Do not invent publication links or imply that unfinished resources are already available.

## 9. Participation and sustainability

Visitors can:
- Explore and question.
- Share ideas and feedback.
- Support the work once or over time.
- Support a particular project.
- Contribute skills and knowledge.
- Collaborate on practical initiatives.
- Seek a deeper conversation or guidance.

Support is intended to make independent work possible, not to create an obligation to agree or participate.

Payment, patronage and collaboration options should only be linked when real destinations and arrangements are configured.

## Editorial principles

- Invitation, not conversion.
- Curiosity, not dogma.
- Root-cause investigation without pretending every hypothesis is established fact.
- Distinguish research, interpretation, models, proposals and implemented experiments.
- No forced linear journey between the disciplines.
- Do not overload the homepage with terminology.
- Preserve the user's project names and conceptual distinctions.
- Explain unfamiliar terms in ordinary language before using them as navigation labels.
- Never invent external links, publications, contact details or operational status.

## V2 acceptance criteria

1. A first-time visitor can explain what atotsm is.
2. The relationship between Usurpia and Arcadia is understandable.
3. Usurology and Arcadialogy / Ikigaiology are clearly distinguished and shown as mutually enriching.
4. The personal-to-collective development pathway makes sense.
5. The Sovereign Library and Sovereign Laboratory have distinct, accurate roles.
6. AIology has a clear place in the ecosystem.
7. The homepage tells a coherent story without becoming an encyclopaedia.
8. Every navigation link leads to the section its label promises.
9. Existing mobile responsiveness and accessibility are preserved or improved.
10. V1 remains recoverable through its Git tag.
