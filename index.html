THE STARTING QUESTION

What if seemingly separate problems share underlying dynamics?

We often approach the world one problem at a time: a difficulty here, a crisis there, a new policy or a temporary fix. But what shapes the conditions in which these problems arise? Which assumptions, incentives and structures influence what we can see, choose and change?

atotsm grew from the desire to investigate roots as well as symptoms — and to connect that investigation with the possibility of creating something better.

Not geographical places or separate societies. Two ways of describing conditions that can coexist within people, relationships and the world around us.

What remains unseen can continue shaping what we experience.

Usurpia describes a condition in which the dynamics of usury and their consequences remain insufficiently recognised. It is an invitation to investigate structures, incentives, assumptions and their wider effects — not a label for a population or a physical place.

LOOKING BENEATH THE SURFACE

More than the ordinary question of interest rates.

Usurology investigates usury as a wider systemic dynamic. It asks how promises, pressures, dependencies and consequences can interact — and why those dynamics may remain difficult to recognise.

Its developing vocabulary includes Persistent Usury Unawareness, the PPPPP lens — Promise, Pressure, Parasitism, Pathologization and Poison — The Lens and The Paradigms, The Flux and the Emergineering, and the 7/8 Dimensional Impact.

One metaphor explores usury as a rocket booster: acceleration can create possibilities, but balance and timely disengagement matter. These are investigative models and interpretations to explore, not conclusions visitors are asked to accept without examination.

A SMALL, CONCRETE EXAMPLE

What does compounding change?

A simplified illustration can make one financial mechanism easier to see. It does not, by itself, prove a wider claim about the monetary system.

STARTING AMOUNT €1,000
ILLUSTRATIVE RATE 5% yearly
PERIOD 10 years
Simple interest €1,500.00

The same €50 interest each year, without adding interest to the balance.

Annual compounding €1,628.89

Each year's interest is added to the balance used to calculate the next year's interest.

Difference in this illustration €128.89

The calculation

With annual compounding and no repayments: €1,000 × 1.0510 = approximately €1,628.89. With simple interest: €1,000 + (€50 × 10) = €1,500.

Important limits: This is deliberately simplified. It assumes no repayments, fees, changing rates or other loan conditions. Real loans differ, and this is not a typical repayment schedule. Interest is not automatically equivalent to usury, and this calculation is not proof of a broad systemic thesis.

Questions worth investigating

  • Who sets the terms, and who has the power to negotiate them?
  • How are risks, rewards and obligations distributed?
  • How do income, circumstances and wider economic conditions affect the outcome?
  • What other arrangements might meet the underlying need?

The point is not to dictate an answer. It is to make a mechanism visible and open it to careful investigation.

A FEW WORDS USED HERE

A small guide to the language.

Some terms are original conceptual frameworks. Their meanings are introduced here so that visitors can question them rather than having to guess.

Usurpia

A name for a condition in which the dynamics of usury and their consequences remain insufficiently recognised. It describes a condition, not a geographical place or a category of people.

Arcadia

A name for the possibility of increasing awareness, human flourishing, cooperation and practical alternatives. It is something that can begin in everyday life, not a promised perfect society.

Usurology

An interdisciplinary investigation of usury as a systemic phenomenon, including its structures, dynamics, consequences and persistent unawareness.

Arcadialogy / Ikigaiology

An open philosophy of purpose, talents, interests, knowledge, capabilities and human flourishing. It explores how people can develop and connect what matters to them.

Complementary currencies

Ways of recognising or exchanging value alongside conventional money. They can be designed around particular communities, purposes, resources or contributions; no single model suits every situation.

Exodus

In this project, a metaphor for awakening and moving towards greater awareness and alternatives here and now — not a call for physical migration away from society.

AI & APPLIED RESEARCH

AIology

How can artificial intelligence become a useful instrument for human investigation, reasoning, experimentation and creation? AIology explores methods of interaction, reasoning methodologies and human–AI collaboration.

One separate applied-research area concerns the Bende van Nijvel cold case: exploring how AI-assisted methods may help organise information, examine reasoning and support the investigation of complex evidence. This case-study work is distinct from the monetary and social-system inquiries elsewhere on this page. Any such work must remain open to scrutiny, uncertainty and human responsibility.
